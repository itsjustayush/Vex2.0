# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Vex is intended to be a welcoming project for people of every background and level of experience. Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, patient, and considerate in all project spaces.

## Expected behavior

Use inclusive language, assume good intent while addressing impact, give actionable feedback, respect different viewpoints, and protect private information. Keep discussions focused on improving the product and its users’ experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable. Do not submit malicious code, credentials, tracking scripts, or content that compromises user privacy.

## Enforcement

Report safety or conduct concerns privately to [info.cometlabs@gmail.com](mailto:info.cometlabs@gmail.com). Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled as confidentially as practical. Project maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
