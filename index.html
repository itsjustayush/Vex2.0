✦ architecture & capabilities

How does Vex elevate personal writing and creative thought?

Vex is designed for thinkers, writers, and builders who need both the precision of structured Markdown notes and the spatial freedom of infinite moodboards.

How does the tactile keyboard sound simulation work?

Keystrokes trigger calibrated mechanical sound sprites through the browser Web Audio API, synthesizing the tactile acoustic resonance of physical typewriter switches. The sound can be dynamically adjusted or muted via the audio control toggle.

How do endless moodboards organize visual concepts?

The infinite 2D canvas lets creators drag images, videos, and color-coded sticky notes into freeform clusters. Users can zoom from 25% to 200% and pan smoothly to discover organic associations between fragments of thought.

How does Markdown and LaTeX formatting work in Vex?

Vex supports standard Markdown syntax for headings, blockquotes, bullet lists, bold, italics, and inline code. Mathematical expressions enclosed in dollar signs (such as $E = mc^2$) render instantly with crisp typographic formatting.

How does cross-device cloud synchronization maintain privacy?

You can write immediately in guest mode without creating an account; your work remains strictly in local client memory. When you sign in via Google or email verification, documents sync securely to Google Cloud Firestore with automatic version history.

What display themes and paper textures are available?

Vex features three curated themes: Dark, Light, and Zen. The writing canvas can also be styled with five paper styles: ruled single, ruled double, plain, dotted light, and dotted dense to suit every writing state of mind.

How does the built-in typing practice mode work?

Enhance Typing tracks your real-time words-per-minute speed, accuracy, and typing cadence over curated literary excerpts, developing keyboard fluency and tactile muscle memory.
